# HDR environments

- `urban-street.hdr`: Urban Street 03, Andreas Mischok, Poly Haven, 1K HDR.
  https://polyhaven.com/a/urban_street_03
- `daylight-sky.hdr`: Kloppenheim 06 (Pure Sky), Greg Zaal / Jarod Guest, Poly Haven, 2K HDR.
  https://polyhaven.com/a/kloppenheim_06_puresky

Both assets are CC0: https://polyhaven.com/license
Files are served locally; the page makes no runtime request to these providers.
The sky panorama is the background only. Buildings, car, pedestrians, vegetation and street furniture remain 3D geometry.
