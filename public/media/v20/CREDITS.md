# Local optimized assets

- daylight-sky.hdr: Kloppenheim 06 Pure Sky, Greg Zaal / Jarod Guest, Poly Haven. CC0. Resized from 2K to 1K, retaining HDR data.
  https://polyhaven.com/a/kloppenheim_06_puresky
- urban-street.hdr: Urban Street 03, Andreas Mischok, Poly Haven. CC0. Resized from 1K to 512 px, retaining HDR data.

Change the versioned directory and source URLs whenever replacing a cached asset.
